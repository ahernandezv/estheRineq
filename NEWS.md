# estheRineq 0.1.0

* Initial release of `estheRineq` (*Epidemiological Statistics Toolkit for Health Inequality Estimation and Reporting in R*), companion R package to Python `esther-ineq`.
* **Core survey & surveillance containers:** `survey_health_data()` (with native adapter for `survey::svydesign` and `survey::svyrep.design`) and `grouped_health_data()`, with zero external hard package dependencies beyond base R.
* **Exact influence-function (`IF` / `RIF`) variance kernel:** $O(N \log N)$ Gâteaux influence functions, stratified ultimate-cluster Taylor linearization, Kakwani-Wagstaff-van Doorslaer convenient regression, Newey-West rank autocorrelation, survey replicate weights, and Rao-Wu-Yue PSU survey bootstrap.
* **Inequality & surveillance suite:** Standard, generalized, modified, Wagstaff, Erreygers, extended, and symmetric concentration indices + omnibus subgroup Wald $F$-tests; concentration and Lorenz curves with simultaneous bands; classical `Gini`, `S-Gini`, `Atkinson` (+ `EDE`), `Generalized Entropy` (`Theil`, `MLD`, and Shorrocks subgroup decomposition), `Palma`, and `S80/S20`; all 21 WHO Health Inequality Monitor (`healthequal` / HEAT) summary measures; and logistic G-computation, Moreno-Betancur, and linear `SII`/`RII`.
* **Standardization & decompositions:** Direct (`DSR`) and indirect (`SMR`, `ISR`) rate standardization with Dobson and Byar Poisson-Gamma CIs and built-in WHO/Segi/European standard populations; Gravelle-consistent indirect health-need standardization and Horizontal Inequity (`HI`); reference-invariant Wagstaff, bivariate `RIF-I-OLS`, and counterfactual `RIF-Oaxaca` decompositions.
* **Editorial visualizations & `STROBE-Equity` reporting:** Five plot families (`equiplot`, `plot_curve`, `plot_sii_gradient`, `plot_decomposition`, `funnel_plot`) supporting both `ggplot2` and native base-R graphics with strict sentence-case typography and dynamic variable names, plus trilingual (`en`, `es`, `pt`) exports to `Markdown`, `LaTeX`, `HTML`, `Excel (.xlsx)`, and `Word (.docx)`.
